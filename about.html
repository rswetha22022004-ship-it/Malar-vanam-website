About Malar Vanam

Discover our passion for flowers and creativity

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Blooming Petals began with a simple dream: to spread happiness through fresh and beautiful flowers. What started as a small flower shop has grown into a trusted destination for bouquets, garlands, and floral decorations.

Our Mission

We believe that flowers speak the language of love, joy, and celebration. Our mission is to deliver fresh, handpicked flowers that brighten every special moment in your life.

Fresh & Quality Flowers

We source our flowers directly from trusted local farms to ensure freshness and fragrance. From roses and jasmine to traditional garlands, every arrangement is crafted with care and attention.

Our Floral Designers

Our skilled floral designers bring creativity and elegance to every bouquet. Whether it's a wedding, birthday, or festival, we design arrangements that leave a lasting impression.
